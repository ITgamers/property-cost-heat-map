Closing

Texas taxes are paid in arrears, so closing later means a much larger escrow deposit — and a matching seller credit. The two cancel: net cash lands about 2.5 months of tax either way. Useful for reading your closing disclosure, not for picking a month.
Builders often cover closing costs if you use their preferred lender. Check the rate before taking it.
